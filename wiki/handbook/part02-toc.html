A note on figures Every diagram in this book was drawn for it. Where the wiki carried a generic box-and-arrow graph, the figure here tries instead to show the mechanism — the thing that actually moves, the state that actually changes, the failure the design is holding off. If a figure does not tell you something the surrounding prose left out, it has not earned its ink.
